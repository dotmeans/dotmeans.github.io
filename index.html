Quick answers

Is income up to ₹12 lakh really tax free in FY 2026-27?

Under the new regime, yes for resident individuals: taxable income up to ₹12 lakh gets a Section 87A rebate of up to ₹60,000, which cancels the tax. With the ₹75,000 standard deduction, a salaried person can have gross salary up to ₹12.75 lakh and still pay nil tax. Slightly above ₹12 lakh, marginal relief limits your tax to the amount by which your income exceeds ₹12 lakh.

What are the new regime slabs for FY 2026-27?

Nil up to ₹4 lakh, 5% from ₹4 to 8 lakh, 10% from ₹8 to 12 lakh, 15% from ₹12 to 16 lakh, 20% from ₹16 to 20 lakh, 25% from ₹20 to 24 lakh, and 30% above ₹24 lakh, plus 4% health and education cess. Budget 2026 did not change these.

When does the old regime win?

When your total deductions and exemptions (HRA, 80C, 80D, NPS, home loan interest and so on) are large enough to offset the old regime's higher slab rates. The calculator above also tells you roughly how much extra deduction the old regime would need to beat the new one.

Can I switch between regimes?

Salaried employees can generally choose a regime each year. Taxpayers with business income have tighter rules on switching back, so check with a professional if that applies to you.
